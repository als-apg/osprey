The Lattice panel has its own toolbar: a sidebar toggle, a model switcher,
Refresh, Baseline and Verify. The tile bar shows only the model name, and the
figure layout (Stacked or Grid) is in ⋯ › This panel. The control sidebar is
open on wide tiles and has a Magnets | Settings switch at its top. A Resonance
figure that a single-pass model cannot draw offers a switch to a periodic
model.
