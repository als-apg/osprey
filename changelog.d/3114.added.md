An application's backend can read and search a qmd corpus. A `runtime: osprey`
container lists the corpora it needs under `containers.<key>.corpora` in
`app.yml`; each is bound read-only into the container, and
`osprey.apps.sdk.corpus(name)` gives its directory (`path`) and its search
(`search(query, limit=...)`, raising `CorpusUnavailable` while the sidecar is
down). `osprey validate` and `osprey build` refuse a name `services.qmd.corpora`
does not declare with a `source`. The plugin's `build-app` skill covers it.
