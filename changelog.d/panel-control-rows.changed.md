A device card's operate panel now draws every control as one row on one set of columns for the whole panel: the kind's tag, the control's name once, what it is now, the entry field or the button (a choice's segments side by side), and for an entry Apply and what it will write; what a group writes follows its rows, under the names. Fields and buttons stand in one column, so none jumps sideways from row to row, and the name no longer stands a second time on a line of its own above the row. The legend of the kinds names only the kinds the panel draws, and stands on the panel's title line when it fits there. Rows that end at their button or value (actions, read-only values) use the width: up to three columns of them stand side by side, filled downward, so a group of many short rows does not run down the page. A group of many entries of one role is still a grid.
