The TRAJECTORY tab now serves an operator panel for first-turn threading: it
opens, takes over and releases a session, runs setup, shots and continuous
shots, shows the step with its unsolved correctors, applies it by its digest,
puts back the values the last apply or setup moved, and offers Stop at any
time. Every refusal is shown with its code, and the panel names what an
action moved before it stopped, since nothing is restored automatically.
