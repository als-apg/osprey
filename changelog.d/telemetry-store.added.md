The presets deploy a telemetry store (ClickHouse) and a telemetry gateway (an OpenTelemetry Collector) under `claude_code.telemetry.backend: gateway`: each sender logs in with its own login, every row carries the sender's name in `User`, and the gateway also reads the audit ledger under `var/audit/` into the table `telemetry.audit_ledger`. Credentials live in `.env.telemetry-*` files of their own, outside the `.env` chain.
