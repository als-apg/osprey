**Breaking change:** `osprey build` and `osprey facility validate` stop with one `profile-invalid` line when a profile ships its own `data/channel_limits.json`, `data/simulation/channel_limits.json` or `project/data/channel_limits.json`; limits are authored in `data/facility/limits.yaml`, which the build renders into the limits database. `osprey mml emit` no longer writes `data/channel_limits.json` (`osprey facility import mml` seeds the export's bands into `limits.yaml`), and the control-assistant preset no longer ships a hand-written limits file.
