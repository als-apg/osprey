The System Health panel has a toolbar with a status filter (All, OK, Warn, Err, Skip, with counts) and a **Scan now** icon. The tooltip of the icon shows the time to the next automatic scan. A **Categories** sidebar lists each check category with its status and count, and a click on a category goes to its card. The tile **⋯** menu has **Expand all details**, **Copy summary** and **Copy on-demand CLI commands**. The panel header, the footer status line and the animated gauge are gone.
