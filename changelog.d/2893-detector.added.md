A facility device that states `acquire` and `results` is a Bluesky detector, named by its device id: a plan triggers it, the trigger waits for the acquire command's status, and each result is recorded under its address. A plan whose devices record one column twice is refused before it writes. The build's plan-device line counts every kind of device. A run describes each column by its channel's value type: text as text, a switch as a boolean, a number as a number; an enum channel is recorded as its word, with its words as the column's choices.
