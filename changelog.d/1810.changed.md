**Breaking change:** `osprey-connectors` declares its public API. Every public
module lists its names in `__all__`, and the `control_system`, `archiver`,
`simulation` and `ipc` subpackages now hide their implementation modules behind
an underscore (`control_system/epics_connector.py` is now
`control_system/_epics_connector.py`, `simulation/engine.py` is now
`simulation/_engine.py`) and export their public names from the package
itself. The old module paths are not kept as aliases.

- Import connector-contract, archiver, simulation and connector-host names from
  `osprey_connectors.control_system`, `.archiver`, `.simulation` and `.ipc`.
  Build a concrete connector through `ConnectorFactory` from its type name; a
  registration that names a built-in by module path uses its new
  `osprey_connectors.control_system._*_connector` or
  `osprey_connectors.archiver._*_archiver_connector` path. `type:` values in
  `config.yml` are unaffected.
- The `osprey.connectors.control_system.*_connector`,
  `osprey.connectors.archiver.*_archiver_connector` and
  `osprey.simulation.{engine,machine,series,expressions,procedural,archiver_seed}`
  paths are removed. `osprey.connectors.control_system.base` and
  `.limits_validator`, and `osprey.connectors.archiver.base` and `._timerange`,
  still import, as their package's namespace.
- Three names lose their underscore: `types.SIMULATED_TYPES`,
  `identity.usable_identity` and `config_writes_enabled` (exported from
  `osprey_connectors.ipc`). `DEFAULT_VA_PORT` moves to `osprey_connectors.types`,
  and the MongoDB store's `address_overrides`, `mongo_client_kwargs` and
  `CA_FILE_ERRORS` are exported from `osprey_connectors.archiver`.
- The package ships a `py.typed` marker, and `lint-imports` now fails the lint
  job if the framework imports past these namespaces or the package imports
  `osprey`.
