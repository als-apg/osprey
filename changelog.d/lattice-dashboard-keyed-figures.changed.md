The lattice dashboard draws every figure a deck's solve allows, whether or not the build serves the model; "not served" is only a label in the model selector. A figure is shown only for the deck, settings, overrides and baseline on screen: switching models, moving a magnet or changing a setting marks the figure stale until it is recomputed, and switching back shows the figure computed before. The agent's figure tools say when a figure is stale, not computed, computing or failed, and `lattice_refresh("da")` and `lattice_refresh("lma")` refresh that figure alone; `lattice_refresh("verify")` runs both.
