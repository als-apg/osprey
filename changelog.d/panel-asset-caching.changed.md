A panel's static files are now stored by the browser and revalidated with an `ETag` (`Cache-Control: no-cache`, answered `304`) instead of being sent whole on every use, text answers over 1 KB leave the web terminal gzipped, and a panel page names the shared style sheets, images and fonts at the shell's fingerprinted immutable address. The rendered nginx config reaches each terminal through a named `upstream` with `keepalive`, and the unauthenticated asset location forwards `Accept-Encoding` and the conditional headers.
