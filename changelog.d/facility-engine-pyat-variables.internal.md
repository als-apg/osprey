The pyat engine gains its own variable classes in `osprey.simulation.engines.pyat_variables`, each built from a wiring record's element or slices, engine block and calibration, and the build stops `engine-invalid` on a non-float channel wired to a pyat model and on a monitor whose calibration has no way back to hardware units; a record naming the deck energy (`attribute: energy`, no element) takes its start value from the deck.
