Each panel tile in the web terminal has one bar: the panel name and subtitle,
up to three panel icons, the shared controls, one **⋯** menu, **Pop out** and
**×**. A click on **⋯** or a right-click on the bar opens the menu. Its
**This panel** section holds the other controls and menu items of the panel,
and its **Tile** section holds the items for the tile. The panel's own **⋯**
button and the **More controls** button on the bar are gone. Panels keep their
own toolbars inside the panel.
