`osprey facility panels write` states each screen's class in `index.json`, so a reviewer judges one standard screen per class and reads how the other members differ. A device's card is classed by its device class, card recipe and the set of signal roles it shows; a set by its members' device types; every other screen by its view kind and structure. Sameness is measured on what a screen shows, not on its pixels: the count of a repeated block, its order, gaps in what is known (a source, a unit, a stated position, a link) and data-state flags are left out. Each entry names its `class`; when it is off its standard, `differs` says how (a section, item kind, block or relation present or missing); `varies` states its other differences in sizes, gaps, flags and drawing variants; and the entry carries its own `sizes`, `gaps`, `flags`, longest `label` and the labels cut to fit (`cuts`). The `classes` table holds each class's key, standard, counts, a layout hash that tells a changed standard, and a `sample` of members that span the class; `class_summary` holds the counts a gate reads. `panels check` reports a stale class as `stale_class`, and the new `osprey facility panels classes` prints the table, a table per view kind and a one-line summary. The panels format is 2.
