A device card's default key tiles now take a role that several of the device's own channels play, as one figure over them all: for a lamp, how many are true, titled with the role's word and the word stated for the true state (`state_labels.csv` or the channel's `options`; "on" only for a `bool` channel, and no tile, named by `check`, where neither holds); for a value, the highest, titled "Highest" and the role's word. A role shown as a word gets no tile, and a stated card row for the class wins over the default. `metrics.csv` card rows take `min`, drawn like `max`. A count reads as one: "of N" follows the figure on its baseline instead of standing in the unit slot, and under it a row of small lamps, one per member and named by it, shows which are counted (filled) and which are not (an outline) where they fit the tile. Under the highest or the lowest, one line names the member that holds it ("highest of N · <name>", its name short of the device's id): one label per member, parked off the page and moved into the line by a geometry rule while its member holds the extreme among the members that report (a tie goes to the first), so a page whose channels have not answered shows none, at most 32 members, past which the line says "highest of N" and `check` names the tile. A name too long for the line is cut so the part that tells members apart survives, and each line's tooltip is the member's whole name. While a member has no value, "one of N has no value" stands beside the holder's name, and the highest or the lowest shows that of the members that report, drawn muted, where its formula fits the engine's length limit (else a dash); a sum stays a dash. A count no longer counts a member with no value. The figure's tooltip names its members. The line under a figure keeps a margin above the tile's border. The font table now also holds the font's ascent, from which the layout puts two texts of different sizes on one baseline. A test holds every rule the generator writes to the props its widget reads in the pinned viewer (the table `RULE_TARGETS`, read from viewer runtime 4038c44), so no rule is dropped unread.
