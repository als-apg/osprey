A device card's default key tiles now take a role that several of the device's own channels play, as one figure over them all: for a lamp, how many are true, titled with the role's word and the word stated for the true state (`state_labels.csv` or the channel's `options`; "on" only for a `bool` channel, and no tile, named by `check`, where neither holds); for a value, the highest, titled "Highest" and the role's word. A role shown as a word gets no tile, and a stated card row for the class wins over the default. `metrics.csv` card rows take `min`, drawn like `max`. A count reads as one: "of N" follows the figure on its baseline instead of standing in the unit slot, and under it a row of small lamps, one per member, shows which are true where they fit the tile. Under the highest or the lowest, one line names the member that holds it ("highest of N · <name>", its name short of the device's id): one hidden label and one rule per member, at most 32 members, past which the line says "highest of N" and `check` names the tile. The figure's tooltip names its members. The line under a figure keeps a margin above the tile's border. The font table now also holds the font's ascent, from which the layout puts two texts of different sizes on one baseline.
