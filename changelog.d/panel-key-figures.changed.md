A device card's default key tiles now take a role that several of the device's own channels play, as one figure over them all: for a lamp, how many are true, titled with the role's word and the word `state_labels.csv` states for the true state (else "on"); for a value, the highest, titled "Highest" and the role's word. A role shown as a word gets no tile, and a stated card row for the class wins over the default. A count reads as one: "of N" follows the figure on its baseline instead of standing in the unit slot. Under the highest, one line names the member that holds it ("highest of N · <name>", its name short of the device's id), from one rule per member, so the cost is one rule per member on top of the figure's formula. The figure's tooltip names its members. The line under a figure keeps a margin above the tile's border. The font table now also holds the font's ascent, from which the layout puts two texts of different sizes on one baseline.
