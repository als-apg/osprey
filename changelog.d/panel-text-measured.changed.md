`osprey facility panels write` measures text in the viewer's font (IBM Plex Sans, from a table of its advance widths) instead of a fixed width per character, and no label leaves its box: a row's label column takes its section's longest label, a chain block's value column takes what its lines show and its labels the rest, a long block label takes two lines, and a text still too long is cut with an ellipsis and listed under `cuts`. The header's line stops before the model stamp; a rack's inferred mark has its own room beside the light; a dipole drawn near the end of a card's strip is cut at the strip's end; and a plot's title is a label of the page, since the viewer draws a plot's own title in a weight the font set does not load.
