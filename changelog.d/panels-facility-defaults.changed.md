Panels: what the facility file states is the default and the rule tables only add to it. A setpoint's readback is its channel's `pair`, a thing's short name what `names.csv` states, else its `label`, else the last part of its id, a channel is drawn by its `role` (setpoint as an entry, readback as a value), and a channel on a device that states a `signal` plays that signal as its role on the card. A `channel_roles.csv` row's stated `label` is now the word on every tile, value row and group title of its channels, and stays in front of what tells two of them apart. Every rule table may now be absent. `check` notes, per table, the rows that only restate the facility file or these defaults (`redundant: <table>: <n> rows ...`).
