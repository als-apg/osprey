Panels: what the facility file states is the default and the rule tables only add to it. A setpoint's readback is its channel's `pair`, a thing's short name its `label`, a channel is drawn by its `role` (setpoint as an entry, readback as a value), and a channel on a device that states a `signal` plays that signal as its role on the card. Every rule table may now be absent. `check` notes, per table, the rows that only restate the facility file or these defaults (`redundant: <table>: <n> rows ...`).
