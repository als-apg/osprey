A rack screen keeps everything of the rack in one column beside its elevation, never under it: from the top, the devices at no unit as a grid of three columns, those that carry a light first; then the classes, the counts and the sources as reference; then, at the column's foot, the devices too many side by side at one unit, level with that unit (or as near under it as the column allows) and joined to it by a line. Where the column holds more than the rack is tall, it runs on in the same column; the rack never grows. Every unit from the rack's floor to its highest stated one is numbered, those a device is stated at in the page's text and the rest muted, and a unit no device takes is drawn as a faint slot, so a gap reads as a free unit. Nothing in the facility states a rack's height yet, so the rack is drawn to its highest stated unit and says so once. No chassis moves from its stated unit, half or height.
