A rack screen keeps everything of the rack beside its elevation, not under it: the devices too many side by side at one unit are listed beside that unit and joined to it by a line, the devices at no unit follow with those that carry a light first, and the counts and sources come last as reference. Every unit from the rack's floor to its highest stated one is numbered, those a device is stated at in the page's text and the rest muted, and a unit no device takes is drawn as a faint slot, so a gap reads as a free unit. Nothing in the facility states a rack's height yet, so the rack is drawn to its highest stated unit and says so once. No chassis moves from its stated unit, half or height.
