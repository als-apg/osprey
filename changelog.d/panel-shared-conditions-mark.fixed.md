On a calibration table, conditions that several rows share no longer read as the last row's note. The shared line sat directly under the last row, in the same muted grey and with no gap, and that row was often not one of those it holds for. It now stands after half a row of space and a thin rule, and a reference mark (1, then 2 for a second shared text) starts the line and follows the title of each row it holds for.
