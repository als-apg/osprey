A chat bridge posts only what the agent made for the person who asked: a
plot, a document, a file saved with `save_artifact()` or registered on purpose,
and the agent's `submit_response` text. It no longer posts the records kept on
the way: the approval preview, notebook and result record of every `execute`
call, and a tool's data record (an `archiver_read` series, an oversized
`channel_read` reading, a screenshot or Phoebus snapshot record, a
`pyaml_measure` file). A dispatch run's `artifacts` list and
`GET /dispatch/{id}/artifacts` leave those out; each is still in the store
and served by id.
