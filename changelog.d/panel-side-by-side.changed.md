Two section frames in a row whose content each needs at most half the width now stand side by side, at the height of the taller one, instead of one under the other at full width. A frame whose content is wider, or holds a widget a rule places, stays full width. Screens with several short sections (a device's operate and channel panels, a channel list's groups, a set's links) get shorter and are drawn larger. Content that stretches (a curve's plot) is drawn after the frame that follows it; where that frame's fixed content takes at most half the width, the plot takes the other half and grows to the frame's height, so a magnet's end view and its excitation curve stand side by side. A drawing now takes at most a half frame's width.
