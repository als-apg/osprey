Channel Finder has one toolbar, the same in a tile and standalone: the sidebar
button and **Explore | Feedback** at its left, the result count and the search
box at its right. The filters are in a sidebar that you show and hide from the
toolbar, and row density, columns and the two clear actions are in the tile's
**⋯** menu under **This panel**, or in the toolbar's **⋯** when the panel runs
standalone. A device you open shows under the results as one wide pane that
you resize by height; the results keep scrolling above it, and each channel's
address stays on one line.
