A turn-by-turn ring of the Virtual Accelerator takes an aperture per element.
The new `apertures` list of the `turn_by_turn` settings states a chamber for
the elements of a pyAT class, for a named deck element or for a range along
the ring, beside the ring-wide `aperture`; a rectangular aperture may state
`[low, high]` limits per plane, so a chamber that opens to one side is stated
as it is. A shot and the trajectory response both track through them.
