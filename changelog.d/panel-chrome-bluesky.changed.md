The **Bluesky** panel has one toolbar, embedded and standalone: a sidebar
toggle, a **Plans** | **Queue** | **Results** switch, the lane picker when there
is more than one lane, a queue status chip that opens its details, **Start** on
the **Queue** view, and **Stop** and **Abort**, which follow the same rules as
before. The plan list with its filter is a sidebar that you can resize and hide.
**Clear waiting plans** and **Clear history** are in the tile **⋯** menu (or
the page's own **⋯** when the panel is not in a tile), and each asks you to
confirm at the top of its card. A plan's description and status are behind an
Info icon, field help is the tooltip of an Info icon beside the label, and
**Reset form** is an icon. While the queue server connects or is unreachable,
the panel shows a centred message with **Retry**.
