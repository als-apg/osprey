The web terminal has an OSPREY logo: a pixel osprey drawn on a 16 px grid. It
is the browser tab icon, on a light tile so that it shows on light and dark
tab bars. It also sits to the left of the OSPREY name in the header. In the
Simple view the osprey beats its wings on the "Working…" line while the agent
works, and it replaces the star on the reasoning row. When the agent stops
thinking, the osprey beats its wings twice and does a full roll. History that
the view loads again does not play the animation, and the reduced-motion
setting stops all of it.
