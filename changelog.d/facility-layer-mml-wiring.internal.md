Add the mml layer's wiring pass, `osprey.facility.layers.mml.wiring`, run by the layer's importer: each imported model's served deck is written under `imported/mml/decks/<model>.json` and its `models.yaml` entry gains `deck` and `wiring`, one wiring record per wired address with the element, calibration and energy scaling its export states. An export that disagrees with its mapping or its deck stops the import with `import mml: export-invalid`, and a wired address no channel record carries with `import mml: reference-missing`; a stop writes nothing.
