The Artifacts tile shows only the artifact: its bar names it and has an
**Info** icon (description, created time, size, file path) and a **Download**
icon, and **⋯** holds the logbook, print, pin and delete actions. A strip of
controls shows over the artifact when you point at it (zoom, pan and
**Export PNG** for a plot in place of the Plotly toolbar, **Copy table** and
**Export CSV** for a table, **Contents**, **Find in note** and **Copy as
Markdown** for a note), and **Pop out** opens on the artifact that the tile
shows.
