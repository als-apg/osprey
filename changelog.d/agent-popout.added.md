The Simple view's Agent pane pops out into its own browser window, like a
service panel: from the Pop out button in its tile bar, the **Pop out** row of
its sidebar menu, or **Pop out Agent** in the command palette. The window is
the chat alone. While it is open the pane's tile leaves the workspace and the
terminal's own console sleeps; the Agent row shows the popped-out state, its
badge counts the approvals waiting in the window, a click on it brings the
window to the front, Channel Finder pastes land in the window, and links in
answers open their panels in the terminal. Closing the window, or **Bring
back** from the row menu, puts the pane back; a switch to the Expert view
brings it back first. The Expert view offers no pop-out for the Agent pane.
