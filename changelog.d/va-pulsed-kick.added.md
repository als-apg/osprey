A turn-by-turn ring kicks a shot with pulsed magnets. A wiring record with
`engine: {signal: pulsed_kick, plane: x}` names a deck element and holds the
magnet's setting; `scale` converts it to radians and `pulse` states the share
of the kick on each turn from the first. The kick acts on the tracked shot
only, never on the closed orbit, so an injection kicker's set point moves the
first turns.
