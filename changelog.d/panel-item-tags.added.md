Each widget of a generated panel that draws a view item names it in its `meta` comment: `item: <kind>` (`rack-unit`, `strip-element`, `key-tile`, `chain-block`, `grid-row`), `thing: <id>` where the item stands for one thing, and `order: <n>` where the view states an order. A checker can then tell which item a widget draws and whether the items read in their stated order, without guessing from text and position. Viewers do not read widget comments, so nothing drawn changes. A class's `drawing` digest leaves the comments out, so the tags move no class. On the fixture example the screens grow by about 1.5%.
