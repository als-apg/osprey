A device's place from its `located_in` claims no longer waits on a claim that is contested: a contested claim removes only itself, a claim is coarse only beside an accepted one, and the accepted claims that remain must agree on one place (of a place and a place inside it, the finer). A device whose claims disagree only on the unit of a rack the other claims agree on is now placed in that rack; no claim's unit is copied, and a key with no accepted claim still projects nothing.
