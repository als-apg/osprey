Pin CANOPY to 5ebf367f and record the payload's screen viewer at b9c493d9. A right click on a widget that shows a channel opens a menu about that channel: its name, description, value, time, alarm severity and limits, with entries to copy the name or the value, plot its history in the data browser, add it to the plot, plot it against another channel in the correlation page, and open a page about it. A widget with several channels has one menu with a switcher above the entries. Shift keeps the browser's own menu. The page a menu opens is the new `web.panels.canopy.pv_details_url`; unset, the menu has no such entry.
