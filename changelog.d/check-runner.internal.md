`scripts/check.py` replaces `gate.sh`, `premerge_check.sh`, `ci_check.sh` and `quick_check.sh` as the one declaration of the merge gate, and CI's lint, test, docs, package and frontend-js jobs call it. It carries every step those scripts and jobs ran, the dock-engine bundle and tests included, and leaves out the secret scan (gitleaks), actionlint, a failing linkcheck and the invariant-tests check of the earlier runner; prettier arrives separately.
