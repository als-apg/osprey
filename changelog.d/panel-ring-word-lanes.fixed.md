On a place strip, the state word over a ringed symbol or badge (WARNING, FAULT) no longer runs into its neighbour's or into a neighbouring ring. A badge's stem length was chosen for the badge's own width, not its word's, so two badges of one stem length could stand closer than a word is wide; on the axis, symbols without a ring took a word lane too, and with only two lanes a third close ring went into an occupied one. Now a ringed badge's stem length is chosen for its word, a longer stem clears the word below it, only ringed symbols on the axis take a lane, and a further lane or stem length is opened where the existing ones are taken; the strip grows by one line per lane.
