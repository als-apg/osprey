`osprey build` now refuses, as a `value-invalid` scenario error naming the key, a scenario whose `drivers`, `couple`, `noise`, `archiver`, `overrides` or other block holds a key the simulator view's records do not have, a null, or a value of the wrong type. The view used to skip what it could not read; it now reads each file whole and refuses one that breaks its records, so the build refuses such a scenario before writing it. The framework reads the simulator view's scenario, seed and archiver-event records as the connectors' models rather than by hand, in `sim apply`, the archive rewrite, the session artifacts, scenario withdrawal, the archiver recorder and the facility build's motion and top-off checks.
