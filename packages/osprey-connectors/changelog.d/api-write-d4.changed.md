The write path's modules and constants are renamed. Environment variables, their values and every stored file keep their bytes.

- **`posture_store` is now `write_switch`:** import the verdicts, the owner ladder, the tier rung and the refusal causes from `osprey_connectors.write_switch`. `STATE_DIR_NAME` is `CONTROL_STATE_DIR_NAME`, with that one public home; `control_context.STATE_DIR_NAME` is gone. `SwitchPosition` and `ArmedPosition` move to `control_context`.
- **Launch pin:** `Posture` becomes `LaunchPin`, `POSTURE_SANDBOX`/`POSTURE_WRITES` become `LAUNCH_PIN_SANDBOX`/`LAUNCH_PIN_WRITES`, `LAUNCH_POSTURE_ENV_VAR` becomes `LAUNCH_PIN_ENV_VAR` (still `OSPREY_LAUNCH_POSTURE`), and `launch_posture_stamp(target, posture)` becomes `launch_pin_stamp(target, pin)`.
- **`posture_env` is now `execution_mode`:** `POSTURE_ENV_VAR` becomes `EXECUTION_MODE_ENV_VAR` (still `OSPREY_EXECUTION_MODE`) and `SANDBOX_MODE` becomes `READONLY_MODE` (still `readonly`). `is_readonly_run()` is unchanged.
- **Tests:** `control_context.invalidate_cache()` becomes `osprey_connectors.testing.invalidate_cache()`.
- **Constants made private:** use the words. `OWNER_WEB_TERMINAL`/`OWNER_CONTROLS_SERVER` are `"web_terminal"`/`"controls_server"` (`WriterKind`); `SWITCH_APPLIED`/`SWITCH_REFUSED` are `"applied"`/`"refused"` (`SwitchStatus`); `REPORT_APPLYING`/`REPORT_APPLIED`/`REPORT_FAILED` are `"applying"`/`"applied"`/`"failed"` (`SwitchProgressStatus`); `write_authorization.SELF_ISSUE_SURFACE` is `"write_switch"`. `REPORT_FILE_PREFIX` and `REPORT_FILE_SUFFIX` are private; `REPORT_FILE_GLOB` stays. `SWITCH_POSITIONS` is `get_args(SwitchPosition)`.
- **New names:** `control_context.read_tree_context(tree, owner)` reads one owner's record out of the read-only tree, answering a `TreeContext`. `WritesStatement.of` takes any `VerdictSurface`, which both verdicts are.
